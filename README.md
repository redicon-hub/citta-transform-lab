# Urban Sanctuary Design

Create a new ultra-premium homepage redesign for the Italian brand “Artigiani in Città”, specialized in high-end custom space-saving furniture and transformable interiors.

The current business already has a deep ecommerce catalog and strong SEO visibility for keywords related to “letti a scomparsa”, “arredamento salvaspazio”, “mobili trasformabili”, and custom furniture.

The ecommerce structure must remain active and accessible, but the new homepage must radically shift the first impression from “discount furniture ecommerce” to “high-end consultancy and interior transformation studio”.

The emotional positioning is NOT:
“buy a bed”.

The emotional positioning IS:
“we solve complex space problems with intelligent design and custom production”.

The homepage must communicate:

 architectural intelligence

 bespoke Italian craftsmanship

 luxury urban living

 intelligent space optimization

 premium consultancy

 calm elegance

 trust

 experience

 real transformation of small apartments

Target audience:

 couples 35-50

 owners of expensive small apartments in Milan and Lombardy

 luxury Airbnb investors

 clients living in high-value urban environments

 people struggling with limited space but unwilling to compromise on comfort or aesthetics

Average project values:

 starting around €5,000

 often reaching €10,000+

 premium custom projects

Very important:
Artigiani in Città has internal production and real woodworking craftsmanship.
This must become a core brand asset.

The visual identity should feel like:
80% warm Italian craftsmanship luxury
20% modern minimal architecture

Inspiration references:

 Molteni

 Poliform

 high-end Italian interior studios

 architectural editorial websites

 luxury furniture brands

 premium hospitality aesthetics

The homepage should NOT feel:

 cheap

 noisy

 discount-oriented

 ecommerce-first

 aggressive

 promotional

Remove the feeling of:

 “offerte”

 “sconti”

 countdowns

 hard selling

 clutter

The homepage must feel:

 cinematic

 spacious

 editorial

 elegant

 refined

 calm

 immersive

VISUAL DIRECTION:

 black and white base palette

 warm neutrals

 large typography

 lots of white space

 slow elegant animations

 minimal UI

 strong typography hierarchy

 oversized premium photography

 cinematic transitions

 architecture magazine feeling

IMPORTANT HERO SECTION:

Create a fullscreen cinematic video hero.

Scene direction:
An elegant modern living room in a premium Milan apartment.
A real sofa is placed in front of a custom wall system.
The wall slowly transforms and reveals a REAL high-quality Murphy bed with a thick premium mattress.
The transformation must feel magical, architectural, and luxurious.
The viewer must immediately understand:
“This is NOT a cheap sofa bed.”
“This is intelligent architecture.”

Use slow motion feeling, warm light, premium materials, architectural atmosphere.

Overlay text should be minimal and emotionally strong.

Possible headline directions:

 “Lo spazio non si aumenta. Si progetta meglio.”

 “Quando ogni centimetro conta, serve esperienza.”

 “Soluzioni salvaspazio progettate intorno alla tua casa.”

 “Trasformiamo piccoli spazi in grandi possibilità.”

Primary CTA:

 “Prenota una consulenza salvaspazio”
Secondary CTA:

 “Invia pianta e misure”

 “Guarda i progetti”

IMPORTANT:
The homepage should focus FIRST on consultancy and problem solving.
The ecommerce catalog should appear LATER in the page.

Suggested homepage structure:

 Cinematic hero video

 Problems we solve

 Why choose us

 Real transformation projects

 Internal craftsmanship and production

 Reviews and social proof

 Consultancy process

 Featured ecommerce collections

 Final premium CTA

SECTION: “Problems we solve”
Do NOT present products first.
Present real-life space problems:

 25sqm apartments

 multifunctional rooms

 guest rooms

 home office spaces

 luxury Airbnb optimization

 children rooms with multiple functions

 tiny urban apartments

 difficult layouts

SECTION: “Why choose us”
Highlight:

 internal production

 years of experience

 real consultancy

 custom projects

 Italian craftsmanship

 premium mechanisms

 installation expertise

 architectural approach

SECTION: “Real projects”
Very visual.
Before/after feeling.
Large photography.
Minimal text.
Editorial presentation.

SECTION: “Reviews”
This is a major strategic asset.
Display reviews elegantly with strong social proof.
Use clean luxury styling.

SECTION: “Consultancy process”
Make the process feel high-end and easy:

 We listen

 We analyze your space

 We design the solution

 We produce internally

 We install professionally

IMPORTANT UX RULE:
The homepage must generate:
calls
WhatsApp contacts
consultation requests
floorplan submissions

NOT immediate low-quality ecommerce purchases.

The ecommerce catalog should become:
“the technical exploration layer”
after the emotional trust has already been built.

MENU STRATEGY:
Keep deep ecommerce navigation available.
But visually prioritize:

 projects

 consultancy

 inspiration

 transformations

 craftsmanship

 solutions

MOBILE EXPERIENCE:
Extremely important.
Must feel cinematic and premium on mobile.
Large typography.
Vertical storytelling.
Immersive sections.
Elegant transitions.

Final emotional feeling:
“Artigiani in Città does not simply sell transformable furniture.
They redesign the way people live in limited urban spaces.”
